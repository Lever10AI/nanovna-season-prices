Best prices happen nowhere near Black Friday

For the models tracked through 2025, every all-time Amazon low landed in January to June, except the LiteVNA 64’s November flicker. The LiteVNA 62 has no Amazon record, and the 67’s only print is its Nov 10, 2026 launch listing. Chasing the calendar is the wrong strategy for this family.

November moves happen early

The SAA-2N stepped down on Nov 8 and Nov 18 in past years. The LiteVNA 64 stepped to $169 on Nov 7 last year. If you watch prices, watch early to mid November, then only flicker-watch during the window.

The import discount now depends on the model

In the duty era the AliExpress gap has closed to nothing on the H and the LiteVNA 64, sits at about $30 on the F V2, and is still enormous on the F V3 and LiteVNA 67. Check both channels, every time.
